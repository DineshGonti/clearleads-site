FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is the data?

We triple-verify every email and target 97%+ accuracy with under 3% bounce. Any records that slip through verification are replaced.

What fields do I get?

Typically full name, job title, company, company size, industry, verified business email, LinkedIn URL, and location — plus any custom fields your campaigns need.

Is the data GDPR-compliant?

We source business contact data on a legitimate-interest basis and provide only professional, role-based information. You remain the controller for how it's used in outreach.

Can this lead into a full campaign?

Yes — many clients start with a list, then have us run the outreach on it. It's the lowest-risk way to see how our data and process perform.
