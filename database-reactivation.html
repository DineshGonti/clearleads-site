Database Reactivation

Database reactivation that revives cold and dormant leads

Database reactivation is a service that turns your dormant leads into new sales meetings. ClearLeads takes the old, cold, and unconverted contacts already in your CRM, cleans and re-verifies them, and runs fresh, relevant multi-channel sequences to re-engage buyers whose timing may finally be right — pipeline from a list you've already paid for.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Why reactivate instead of buying new leads?

Contacts already in your database know your brand and once showed interest, so they convert cheaper and faster than cold-sourced leads. Reactivation typically costs around 60% less per meeting.

Won't these leads be annoyed to hear from us?

Not when the message is relevant. We lead with a genuine reason to reconnect — a new capability, a changed price, a relevant result — and always honour opt-outs.

What if the data is really old?

We re-verify and cleanse first, dropping dead records and updating what we can, so you're only messaging reachable, valid contacts.

How fast does reactivation work?

Because there's no list-building lag, campaigns can launch within days and produce meetings in the first two to three weeks.
