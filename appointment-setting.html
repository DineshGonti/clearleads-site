FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does appointment setting cost?

Most B2B appointment-setting engagements run on a monthly retainer between roughly $2,000 and $6,000, sometimes with a per-meeting or performance component. Pricing depends on target volume, market complexity, and how many channels you run. We give you a fixed quote and a realistic meeting forecast on your strategy call. See our full breakdown in How much does appointment setting cost?

What counts as a qualified meeting?

A meeting with a decision-maker who matches your agreed ICP and has expressed genuine interest in your offer. We set the criteria — title, company size, budget, timeline — with you before launch, so every booked meeting is one worth taking.

What happens if a prospect no-shows?

We track show-ups and follow up to reschedule. No-shows caused by factors within our control are replaced at no extra cost, so you only pay for meetings that count.

Do the meetings appear on our own calendar?

Yes. We book directly into your calendar (Calendly, Google, or Outlook) with full context notes on the prospect and the conversation, so your rep walks in prepared.

Is it better to outsource appointment setting or hire SDRs?

Outsourcing is usually faster and cheaper: a fully-loaded in-house SDR often costs $90k–$160k a year and takes about three months to ramp, while an agency is typically live in two to four weeks for a fraction of the cost. Read the full breakdown in Outsourced SDR vs in-house SDR.

Can you integrate with our CRM?

We sync activity and booked meetings to HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, and most major CRMs so your pipeline stays clean and attributable.
