How it works

How ClearLeads books qualified meetings for you

A transparent, four-stage system. You approve the strategy and take the meetings — we build the engine, run the outreach, and prove it with weekly numbers.

Book a call

ClearLeads books meetings in four stages: discovery, build, launch, and optimise. We define your ICP and offer, stand up warmed sending infrastructure with verified lists and copy, launch multi-channel outreach with human reply handling, and then report and optimise weekly — typically going live within 14 days.

1

Discovery & ICP

We run a kickoff to define your ideal customer profile, sharpen your positioning, and agree exactly what a 'qualified meeting' means for you. This is where good outbound is won or lost.

  • Ideal customer profile & target accounts
  • Offer and messaging positioning
  • Qualification criteria agreed
  • Meeting forecast set
2

Build & infrastructure

We stand up dedicated sending infrastructure, warm the inboxes, build your verified prospect list, and write channel-specific copy. Everything is engineered for the primary inbox.

  • Dedicated domains & inboxes warmed
  • SPF/DKIM/DMARC authenticated
  • Verified ICP prospect list
  • Email & LinkedIn copy written and tested
3

Launch & book

Sequences go live across email and LinkedIn. Our team handles every reply — questions, objections, scheduling — and books qualified meetings straight to your calendar with full context.

  • Multi-channel sequences live
  • Human reply handling & qualification
  • Meetings booked to your calendar
  • Context notes for every meeting
4

Optimise & scale

We report weekly on real numbers, A/B test copy and lists, retire what underperforms, and scale what converts — so cost per meeting drops and pipeline compounds month over month.

  • Weekly reporting & numbers call
  • A/B testing of copy and lists
  • Continuous deliverability monitoring
  • Scale winning segments

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long until the system is live?

About 14 days. The main dependency is inbox warm-up, which can't be rushed without hurting deliverability. Strategy, lists, and copy are built in parallel during that window.

What do you need from us to start?

An intro call to define your ICP and offer, access or details for calendar and CRM, and a quick approval of the messaging. From there we run the machine.

How involved do we need to be?

Lightly. You approve strategy and messaging up front and take the meetings we book. We handle prospecting, sending, and reply management day to day, and report weekly.

How do you report progress?

You get a live dashboard plus a weekly numbers call covering sends, reply rates, and meetings booked — with next-step optimisations.

Ready to fill your calendar with qualified meetings?

Book a 30-minute strategy call. We'll map your ICP, show you the plan, and give you a realistic meeting forecast — no pressure, no long contracts.
