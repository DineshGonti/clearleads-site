FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference from your managed service?

Managed service means we run outbound for you every month. System setup is a one-time build: we construct and document everything, then hand it to your team to operate. No ongoing retainer.

Can you train our SDRs?

Yes. Handover includes written playbooks plus a live training session so your reps can run and optimise the system confidently.

What if we want help later?

Many setup clients bring us back for a managed program or a periodic optimisation review. There's no obligation either way.

How long until we're sending?

The full build takes about two to three weeks, mostly driven by inbox warm-up. After handover your team can send from day one.
