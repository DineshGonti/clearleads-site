FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does a B2B lead generation agency actually do?

A B2B lead generation agency runs outbound prospecting on your behalf — building targeted lists, writing and sending cold email and LinkedIn messages, and booking qualified sales meetings directly onto your calendar. ClearLeads owns the full top-of-funnel so your closers spend their time selling, not prospecting.

How is a “qualified meeting” defined?

A qualified meeting is a scheduled call with a decision-maker who matches your ideal customer profile and has expressed genuine interest in your offer. We agree the qualification criteria with you up front, and no-shows within our control are replaced free.

How quickly will I see booked meetings?

Most clients are live within 14 days and see their first booked meetings inside the first 2–4 weeks, once inbox warm-up and sequencing hit full volume. Pipeline compounds from there as we optimise messaging and lists.

Do I need long-term contracts?

No. After an initial ramp period we operate month-to-month. We'd rather keep your business by booking meetings than by locking you into a contract.

Which industries do you work with?

We focus on B2B companies with a defined ICP and a real sales motion — SaaS, agencies, professional services, recruiting, and B2B services. If you sell to other businesses and know who your buyer is, outbound can work for you.
