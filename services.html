FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between appointment setting and lead generation?

Lead generation is the broad practice of finding and engaging potential buyers. Appointment setting is the outcome-focused end of it: qualifying those prospects and booking them as sales meetings. ClearLeads does both, but we're measured on meetings booked, not leads collected.

Can I buy just one service, like lead lists?

Yes. Lead list building and data is a popular standalone entry point. You can also add cold email, LinkedIn, or database reactivation individually, or combine them into a full outbound system.

Do you replace or complement our in-house SDRs?

Either. Many clients use us as their entire outbound engine; others use us to add capacity, test new segments, or cover channels their SDRs don't run. We integrate with your CRM and calendar either way.
