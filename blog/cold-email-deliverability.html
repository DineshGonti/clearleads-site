Deliverability

Cold email deliverability: how we land in the inbox

By the ClearLeads team · Updated June 2026 · 6 min read

The infrastructure and sending discipline that decides whether your outreach is ever seen.

Cold email deliverability depends on sender reputation, not clever wording. Landing in the primary inbox requires dedicated warmed domains, full SPF/DKIM/DMARC authentication, low sending volume per inbox, rigorously verified lists, and human-sounding copy that earns replies. Get those right and monitor them continuously, and spam placement stops being your bottleneck.

Why cold email lands in spam

Spam placement is rarely about the words in one email. It's about sender reputation: sending from an unwarmed domain, missing authentication, blasting too much volume from one inbox, or triggering spam complaints. Fix the reputation and placement follows.

The setup that keeps you in the inbox

  1. Use dedicated domains. Never send cold email from your primary domain. Register separate lookalike domains so your main reputation is never at risk.
  2. Warm every inbox. Ramp sending gradually over 2–3 weeks so mailbox providers learn the inbox is a real human, not a blaster.
  3. Authenticate properly. Configure SPF, DKIM, and DMARC on every sending domain. Missing records are an instant credibility hit.
  4. Keep volume-per-inbox low. Spread sending across multiple inboxes rather than pushing high volume through one. Low and steady beats high and spiky.
  5. Send to verified lists. Bounces destroy reputation. Triple-verify data and keep bounce rates under 3%.
  6. Write like a human. Short, specific, low-link messages that earn replies. Engagement is itself a positive deliverability signal.

Monitor, don't set-and-forget

Deliverability drifts. We monitor inbox placement, reply rates, and spam signals continuously, rotate inboxes, and pull back volume the moment metrics dip — before it becomes a problem you can see in booked meetings.

The one-line version: dedicated warmed domains + full authentication + low per-inbox volume + verified lists + human copy = the primary inbox.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Why does my cold email go to spam?

Usually because of poor sender reputation: an unwarmed domain, missing SPF/DKIM/DMARC authentication, too much volume from one inbox, or high bounce rates from unverified lists. Fixing the setup fixes placement.

How long does inbox warm-up take?

About two to three weeks. Warm-up gradually increases sending volume so mailbox providers recognise the inbox as legitimate. Skipping it is the fastest way into the spam folder.

Should I send cold email from my main domain?

No. Always use dedicated, separately-registered lookalike domains so that any reputation impact never touches your primary domain and its normal business email.
