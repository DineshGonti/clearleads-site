What is B2B appointment setting? A complete guide

By the ClearLeads team · Updated November 2026 · 6 min read

The process, the people, the price — and how it differs from plain lead generation.

B2B appointment setting is the process of booking qualified sales meetings with decision-makers on your behalf. An appointment setter builds a targeted prospect list, contacts buyers through cold email, LinkedIn, or phone, handles replies, qualifies interest against agreed criteria, and schedules the meeting on your calendar — so your sales team spends its time closing instead of prospecting.

What an appointment setter does

An appointment setter sits at the top of the sales funnel. Their job ends when a qualified prospect has a meeting on a closer's calendar. Day to day that means:

  • Building lists of decision-makers who match the ideal customer profile (ICP).
  • Reaching out by cold email, LinkedIn, and sometimes phone.
  • Handling replies, questions, and objections.
  • Qualifying interest against agreed criteria.
  • Booking the meeting and passing full context to the sales rep.

How the appointment-setting process works

  1. Define the ICP and offer. Who is the buyer, what problem do you solve, and what counts as qualified?
  2. Build the list. Verified contacts at the right companies and seniority.
  3. Set up infrastructure. Dedicated domains, warmed inboxes, and LinkedIn profiles.
  4. Run outreach sequences. Several personalised touches across channels.
  5. Qualify and book. Interested replies are qualified and scheduled onto your calendar.
  6. Report and optimise. Track sends, replies, and meetings, and improve weekly.

In-house setters vs an appointment-setting agency

You can hire SDRs to set appointments, or outsource to an agency that brings the people, data, tooling, and deliverability know-how. Agencies are typically faster to launch and cheaper than a fully-loaded hire — we break the numbers down in Outsourced SDR vs in-house SDR.

Who appointment setting is for

  • B2B companies with a proven offer and a team that can close.
  • Deal sizes that justify the cost of a sales meeting (usually $5,000+ in lifetime value).
  • A definable buyer — a clear title, industry, and company size.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is B2B appointment setting?

B2B appointment setting is the process of finding decision-makers who match your ideal customer profile, contacting them through cold email, LinkedIn, or phone, qualifying their interest, and booking them into sales meetings with your team.

What does an appointment setter do?

An appointment setter builds prospect lists, runs outreach, handles replies and objections, qualifies prospects against agreed criteria, and books meetings directly onto a sales rep's calendar with context notes.

Is appointment setting the same as telemarketing?

No. Modern B2B appointment setting is mostly multi-channel — cold email and LinkedIn first, phone sometimes — and is measured on qualified meetings rather than call volume.

How much does appointment setting cost?

Most B2B appointment-setting agencies charge $2,000–$6,000 per month on retainer, or $100–$400 per qualified meeting on a pay-per-meeting model.
