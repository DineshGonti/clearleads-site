Outsourced SDR vs in-house SDR: cost, ramp time, and results

By the ClearLeads team · Updated November 2026 · 7 min read

What a sales development rep really costs, how fast each option produces meetings, and which fits your stage.

An outsourced SDR agency is usually cheaper and faster than hiring in-house. A fully-loaded US in-house SDR typically costs $90,000–$160,000 a year and takes around three months to ramp, while an outsourced SDR service typically costs $24,000–$72,000 a year, includes tools and data, and books first meetings within two to four weeks. In-house wins on deep product knowledge and long-term control.

The true cost of an in-house SDR

An SDR's salary is only the visible part. Once you add commission, benefits, tools, management time, and the months before they're productive, a single US-based SDR typically costs well into six figures a year.

Fully-loaded total$90,000–$160,000

Then factor in ramp time — usually around three months before a new SDR books at full pace — and turnover: SDR tenure is often under two years, so you pay the hiring and ramp cost again.

The cost of an outsourced SDR agency

An outsourced SDR or appointment-setting agency usually charges a monthly retainer of roughly $2,000–$6,000 ($24,000–$72,000 a year), sometimes with a per-meeting component. That fee normally includes the people, the sending infrastructure, the data, and the tooling.

When in-house makes more sense

  • You have a very technical or regulated product that needs deep, daily product knowledge.
  • You already have a proven outbound playbook and a manager who can coach SDRs.
  • You want to build a long-term sales career path from SDR to AE.

When outsourcing makes more sense

  • You need pipeline in weeks, not quarters.
  • You haven't yet proven which segments and messages work in outbound.
  • You don't want to own domains, inbox warm-up, data, and tooling.
  • Your founders or AEs are still doing their own prospecting.
A common path: outsource first to prove the channel and the messaging, then hire in-house SDRs onto a system that already works — or keep the agency and let your AEs focus purely on closing.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is an outsourced SDR cheaper than hiring one?

Usually, yes. A fully-loaded US in-house SDR typically costs $90,000–$160,000 a year once salary, commission, benefits, tools, and management are included. An outsourced SDR agency usually costs $24,000–$72,000 a year and includes infrastructure, data, and tooling.

How long does an in-house SDR take to ramp?

Most new SDRs take around three months to reach full productivity, on top of the four to eight weeks it often takes to recruit them. An outsourced team is typically live within two to four weeks.

Can an outsourced SDR team work alongside my sales reps?

Yes. The agency books qualified meetings directly onto your reps' calendars and syncs activity to your CRM, so your account executives simply take the calls and close.
