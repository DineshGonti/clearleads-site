What is a good cold email reply rate? 2026 benchmarks

By the ClearLeads team · Updated November 2026 · 5 min read

Realistic numbers for B2B outbound, why open rates mislead, and how to turn replies into meetings.

A good B2B cold email reply rate is 5% or higher, with a positive reply rate of 2% or more. Average campaigns see 2–5% total replies. Open rates are unreliable because of mail privacy features, so track replies, positive replies, and meetings booked. Targeting, deliverability, relevance, a low-friction ask, and consistent follow-ups are the levers that move results.

B2B cold email benchmarks at a glance

MetricWeakAverageStrong
Bounce rate>5%2–5%<2%
Total reply rate<2%2–5%5–10%+
Positive reply rate<0.5%0.5–2%2–4%+
Positive reply → meeting<30%30–50%50%+

These are practical ranges for well-targeted B2B campaigns; your numbers will vary by industry, seniority, and offer. Treat open rates with caution — privacy features in modern mail apps make them unreliable, so replies and meetings are the metrics to manage.

Why reply rate beats open rate

Apple Mail Privacy Protection and image-blocking inflate or hide opens, so an "opened" email may never have been read. Replies and booked meetings can't be faked, which is why we report on those first.

The five levers that move reply rate

  1. Targeting. The single biggest lever. A tight ICP list beats clever copy sent to the wrong people.
  2. Deliverability. If you're in spam, nothing else matters. See how we land in the inbox.
  3. Relevance. Reference a real trigger: a hire, a funding round, a tech change, a stated priority.
  4. A low-friction ask. "Worth a look?" outperforms "Can we book 30 minutes Tuesday?" on a first touch.
  5. Follow-ups. A large share of replies arrive on the second to fourth touch. Most campaigns stop too early.

From reply rate to meetings

A useful rule of thumb: 1,000 well-targeted emails at a 2% positive reply rate and a 50% booking rate produces about 10 meetings. Improve any of the three — list quality, positive reply rate, or how fast replies are handled — and meeting volume compounds.

Fastest win: respond to positive replies within an hour. Speed to reply is one of the cheapest ways to lift the reply-to-meeting rate.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is a good cold email reply rate?

For targeted B2B cold email, a total reply rate of 5% or more is strong, and 2–5% is average. A positive reply rate — people expressing interest — of 2% or higher is a strong result.

What is a good cold email open rate?

Open rates are no longer reliable because of mail privacy features that pre-load images. Focus on reply rate, positive reply rate, and meetings booked instead.

How many cold emails does it take to book a meeting?

As a rough guide, around 100 well-targeted emails per meeting is achievable: 1,000 emails at a 2% positive reply rate with half of those booking produces about 10 meetings.
