FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What happens on the call?

A focused 30 minutes: we learn your offer and ICP, tell you honestly whether outbound is a fit, outline the plan, and give you a realistic meeting forecast and quote. No pressure and no obligation.

How much does it cost to work with ClearLeads?

Most managed programs run on a monthly retainer between roughly $2,000 and $6,000 depending on volume and channels; lead lists and one-time builds are quoted separately. You'll get a fixed quote on the call.

How soon can we start?

Onboarding usually begins within a few days of the call, and campaigns go live in about 14 days once inboxes are warmed.
