Our strategists set the targeting, review the messaging, and step in on every real conversation, so you get AI speed and scale without the robotic, spammy outreach.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is an AI SDR?

An AI SDR (AI sales development representative) is software that does the prospecting work of a human SDR: researching prospects, writing outreach, sending follow-ups, and responding to replies. An agentic AI SDR goes further, planning and carrying out multi-step tasks on its own rather than just filling in templates.

Will prospects know they're talking to AI?

Messages are written to read naturally and are reviewed against your brand voice. Every qualified conversation is handed to a real person on our team before a meeting is booked, so prospects always deal with a human when it matters.

Is an AI SDR better than a human SDR?

AI is faster and more scalable: it researches thousands of prospects, personalises every message, and replies at any hour. Humans are better at judgment, nuance, and closing a conversation. That's why our service combines both: AI agents do the volume, our team handles the conversations that turn into meetings.

Does AI outreach hurt email deliverability?

Not when it's run with guardrails. We send from dedicated warmed domains, cap volume per inbox, check copy for spam signals, and monitor placement continuously. See our deliverability guide for the full setup.

How is this different from your appointment-setting service?

Appointment setting is our human-led, done-for-you program. The AI agentic SDR uses AI agents for research, writing, and follow-up, which lets you cover many more accounts at a lower cost per prospect. Many clients combine the two.
