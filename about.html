About ClearLeads

We build pipeline for B2B teams that would rather be closing

ClearLeads exists because most companies know outbound works but don't have the time, tooling, or patience to run it well. So we run it for them — and we report every number along the way.

Book a call

ClearLeads is a B2B lead generation agency specialising in appointment setting and outbound outreach. We help B2B companies book qualified sales meetings through done-for-you cold email and LinkedIn campaigns, combining verified data, strong messaging, and honest weekly reporting so clients get pipeline they can trust.

Our story

ClearLeads started with a simple frustration: talented sales teams stuck doing their own prospecting, and outbound "experts" hiding behind vanity metrics. We'd seen how much pipeline a well-built outbound system could produce — and how rarely it was actually built well.

So we assembled a team obsessed with two things: landing in the primary inbox, and booking meetings worth taking. No black boxes, no meaningless dashboards. Just clear targeting, clear messaging, and a weekly call where we show you exactly what happened and what's next.

Today we run outbound for B2B SaaS, consultancies, agencies, and service businesses across the globe — and we're still measured on the same single number: qualified meetings booked.
