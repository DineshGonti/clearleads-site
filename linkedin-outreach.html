FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is LinkedIn outreach safe for my account?

Yes. We stay well within LinkedIn's activity limits, use gradual ramp-up, and mimic natural human behaviour. Your account safety is never traded for volume.

Do you post content or just send messages?

Our core service is direct outreach and booking meetings. We can also advise on profile optimisation and light thought-leadership content to lift response rates, but messaging is where the meetings come from.

Do I need Sales Navigator?

It's strongly recommended for precise targeting and higher-quality lists. We'll help you set it up if you don't have it.

Can you run LinkedIn and cold email together?

Absolutely — multi-channel sequences that touch a prospect on both email and LinkedIn consistently book more meetings than either alone.
